RAKSHANET SECURITY PLATFORM
Customer overview | Recorded local evaluation | 23 September 2026

FROM EXPOSURE TO VERIFIED CLOSURE
Intelligence, telemetry, scoped validation and human-governed remediation,
connected around a case and its evidence.

FOUR INDEPENDENT PRODUCTS
SentinelAshoka: threat intelligence, advisory context and source provenance.
TelemetryX: normalized telemetry, inventory, exposure context and case projections.
RakshaNet: scoped validation, preserved evidence and independent retesting.
Sanjeevani: persisted remediation plans, approvals, bounded execution and recovery.

CONNECTED WORKFLOW
1. Put a relevant intelligence signal in asset context.
2. Validate the authorized target to establish exposure.
3. Prepare and approve a bounded remediation plan.
4. Execute, verify locally and independently retest.
5. Close only when the retest confirms the fix; otherwise recover and keep open.

RECORDED EVIDENCE
16 completed passing runs in the reviewed snapshot; 12 checks per run.
The separate 20-consecutive-pass release gate is not certified by this website.
Real dependency repair: CLOSED / FIX_CONFIRMED after independent retest.
Deliberately stale deployment: rollback; exposure remained open.
Safe control: false positive suppressed; no remediation.
Restart persistence, duplicate replay and AI-provider failure were also checked.

LIMITATIONS
The official advisory was curated, not found by a live collector.
Approval was tested with separate role API credentials, not a human click.
TelemetryX coverage used optional inventory and projection adapters, not full
lake or shipper acceptance. Approval and execution still share a service.
This does not establish broad autonomous repair or production readiness.
