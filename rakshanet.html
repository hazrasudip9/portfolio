    What the lab demonstrated

    • Real dependency repair followed by an independent retest
    • Rollback when the deployed target remained vulnerable
    • Safe-control suppression without remediation
    • Preserved cases and consumer cursors after restart
    • Idempotent duplicate delivery and fail-closed AI failure

    What this does not establish

    • Live collector discovery: the advisory was curated.
    • Human approval UX: the test used separate role credentials.
    • Full TelemetryX lake or shipper acceptance.
    • Production isolation: approval and execution still share a service.
    • Broad autonomous remediation across arbitrary environments.
